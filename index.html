Researched tech

Tick a tech to see what researching it would do to alloy/item sell prices, smelt times, and ingredient costs. Base prices never include these bonuses, so a toggle simply means "I've researched this".

Mothership rooms

Enter the level you've purchased for each room (0 = not purchased); the multiplier next to it is the resulting bonus.

Station nodes

Subspace Station tech-node levels (0 = not purchased). Smelting and Crafting boost smelt/craft speed; Value boosts alloy and item sell price. Nodes in a category stack additively into the category total shown next to its name.

Modules

Modules have no fixed level formula (main effects scale unpredictably; sub-effects are randomly rolled), so enter each effect's current value directly. An effect that boosts more than one category (e.g. a module's main effect often boosts both smelt and craft speed) needs one row per category.

Stats

Edit stars, market roll, time, and ingredient amounts inline. Base price and effective sell price are shown but not directly editable — sell price is base price × stars × the bonuses above. Ores have no craft time or ingredients and never appear in the chart. Tick entities on as you unlock them in-game — turning one on also turns on everything it needs; turning one off also turns off anything that needs it.
